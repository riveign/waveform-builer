What if the player's waveform showed where the track lifts?

These are three real tracks from your "Excellent Techno" set, drawn from the waveform data Kiku already stores. The player bar at the bottom works like the real one: play, skip, click the waveform to seek. Pick a style below or with the switch in the bar.

Same waveform, larger

    How each style is drawn

    • Loudness smooths the envelope over about 6 seconds, scales it to the track's own loudest part, then blends teal → amber → red. So a quiet track still shows its own peak in red. Color is relative to the track, not compared across your library.
    • Played vs. still to come: in the colored styles, the part you've heard is full color and the rest is dimmed. The playhead reads without a separate progress color.
    • Spectral reuses the Track view's renderer and palette, so the two views always agree.

    Still to decide

    • Name: "Loudness" is honest, but "Energy" is what a DJ would say. Kiku already scores energy, though, so that name could suggest this is that score.
    • Where the switch lives: in the bar (as here), or only in settings. Either way, your choice would be remembered.
    • Track view too? Its Classic/Spectral toggle could gain Loudness, so both waveforms offer the same three styles.
